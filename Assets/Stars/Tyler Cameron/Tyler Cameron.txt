Real estate is in Tyler Cameron’s blood. He grew up in Jupiter,
Florida, with a real estate agent for a mother and a general
contractor for a father, ultimately finding his own way to blend those
two worlds. Today, he runs Image 1 Construction, his construction
company, while also building a company with his fiancée, Tate Madden,
flipping and designing homes. Cameron leads the construction and
flips, while Madden brings each space to life through design. In 2025,
Cameron earned his real estate license and joined SERHANT as an agent
in Palm Beach County, Florida, bringing his passion for homes,
construction and real estate under one roof.

Most recently, Cameron served as a judge on HGTV’s “Bachelor
Mansion Takeover” and starred in Prime Video’s “Going Home with
Tyler Cameron.” The Emmy®-nominated series followed him back to
Jupiter to renovate homes for locals while working toward his most
personal project: restoring his late mother’s house. That same
instinct for creating — homes, companies and community — carries
through everything he does. Alongside Madden, he is also developing
TINKR, a modern tool brand centered on DIY and empowering people to
feel more confident creating a home they love. Cameron serves as head
of Product, while Madden is head of Creative. His entrepreneurial
interests also extend to health and wellness as an owner and active
partner with 3Natives, a healthy fast-casual franchise in South
Florida.

Cameron co-founded the Andrea C. Cameron Foundation with his brothers
in honor of their late mother, providing scholarships to students
throughout the Palm Beach County community she loved. A former college
athlete, he remains deeply connected to fitness and community through
the Celsius Fitness Tour, where he leads workouts nationwide, as well
as a monthly run club in his hometown.

Cameron first appeared on national television in 2019 on Season 15 of
“The Bachelorette” and has since built an impressive career that
extends far beyond television, spanning entrepreneurship,
construction, real estate and design. His other media credits include
ABC’s “Extreme Makeover: Home Edition,” Fox’s “Special
Forces: World’s Toughest Test” (which he won), “The Real Dirty
Dancing,” Netflix’s “Good on Paper” and an appearance on
“Saturday Night Live.”
