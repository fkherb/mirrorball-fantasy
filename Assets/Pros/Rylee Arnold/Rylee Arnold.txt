Rylee Arnold comes from a family of competitive dancers and is the
younger sister of former DWTS pro Lindsay Arnold. She competed as a
professional mentor on Dancing with the Stars: Juniors before joining
the main show as a pro. Arnold’s background includes ballroom and
Latin competition, as well as years of training and performance in
Utah. Her first seasons as a celebrity partner brought her a large
following, and she has also toured with DWTS. She is one of the show’s
youngest current pros.

CAREER HIGHLIGHTS
DWTS: Juniors mentor; competitive ballroom dancer; DWTS pro since
Season 32.
