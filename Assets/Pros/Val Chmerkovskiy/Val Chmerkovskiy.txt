Val Chmerkovskiy was born in Ukraine and raised in the United States,
where he began ballroom training with his family. He became a multi-
time U.S. national champion and joined DWTS as a pro in Season 13.
Known for technical precision and ambitious choreography, he has also
performed in live dance productions and on Broadway. Chmerkovskiy has
partnered with athletes, actors, and entertainers across many seasons,
winning two Mirrorballs, including with Rumer Willis and Laurie
Hernandez.

CAREER HIGHLIGHTS
U.S. ballroom champion; two-time DWTS Mirrorball winner; Broadway and
touring performer.
