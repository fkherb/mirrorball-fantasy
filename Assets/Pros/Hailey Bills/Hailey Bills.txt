Hailey Bills began dancing at a young age and trained in a wide range
of styles, including ballroom, jazz, contemporary, and hip-hop. She
appeared on Dancing with the Stars: Juniors before joining the main
show’s troupe, where she performed in group numbers and supported the
celebrity-pro routines. Her promotion to the Season 35 pro lineup
gives her a celebrity partnership after years of performing in the
DWTS ensemble. Bills is also known for her connection to the Arnold
and Johnson dance families through her training and work in Utah’s
competitive dance community.

CAREER HIGHLIGHTS
DWTS: Juniors alum; two-time national champion at The Dance Awards;
former DWTS troupe member.
