Alan Bersten was born in Minnesota to a family with Argentine roots
and began dancing as a child, training in ballroom and Latin styles.
He became widely known after competing on So You Think You Can Dance,
then joined DWTS first as a troupe member and later as a pro.
Alongside his work as a performer, he choreographs and has appeared on
the DWTS live tour. On the show, he’s known for energetic routines and
for adapting his choreography to partners with very different levels
of dance experience. Alan won the Mirrorball with Hannah Brown in
Season 28.

CAREER HIGHLIGHTS
Glee performer; So You Think You Can Dance finalist; DWTS troupe alum;
Season 28 Mirrorball winner.
