Ezra Sosa is a ballroom dancer and choreographer from Utah, where he
trained and competed alongside his sister, Stephani Sosa. He became
familiar to DWTS viewers as a troupe member before being promoted to
pro. His background includes performance and choreography across
ballroom, Latin, and contemporary styles, and he has also appeared on
the show’s live tour. On DWTS, Sosa is known for expressive
performance choices and for bringing a contemporary sensibility to
ballroom routines.

CAREER HIGHLIGHTS
Former DWTS troupe member; promoted to pro; ballroom and Latin
competitor.
