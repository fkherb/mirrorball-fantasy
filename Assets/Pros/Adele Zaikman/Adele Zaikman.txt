Adele Zaikman is a Los Angeles–based dancer and teacher with Israeli
and Ukrainian roots. Before appearing on The Next Pro, she built her
career in competitive Latin ballroom, developing the technical
precision and performance quality that became central to her run on
the show. She won the first season of The Next Pro and joined the
Season 35 cast as a professional partner. Her arrival brings a
competitor with a deep ballroom background into a role that also
requires her to teach and choreograph for a celebrity partner.

CAREER HIGHLIGHTS
