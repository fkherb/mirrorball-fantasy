Brandon Armstrong grew up dancing in California and trained in several
styles, including hip-hop and ballroom. He appeared on Dancing with
the Stars: Juniors as a mentor before joining the main show’s troupe,
then became a pro. In addition to partnering celebrities, he has
choreographed for DWTS and toured with the show. His work often blends
ballroom technique with contemporary and street-dance influences, and
he has become a familiar part of the show’s pro ensemble over multiple
seasons.

CAREER HIGHLIGHTS
DWTS: Juniors mentor; former DWTS troupe member; DWTS pro and
choreographer.
