Britt Stewart is a Denver-born dancer and choreographer who trained in
ballet, jazz, hip-hop, and ballroom. Before becoming a DWTS pro, she
toured with major stage productions and appeared as a dancer in the
television series Glee. She joined DWTS as a troupe member and later
became the show’s first Black female pro. Stewart brings a broad
performance background to the ballroom, with choreography that draws
on both her formal dance training and her commercial and theater work.

CAREER HIGHLIGHTS
Glee performer; High School Musical performer; DWTS troupe alum; first
Black woman to serve as a DWTS pro.
