Jenna Johnson grew up in Utah and trained in Latin and ballroom dance,
as well as contemporary styles. She first reached a national
television audience as a contestant on So You Think You Can Dance,
then joined DWTS as a troupe member before becoming a pro. Johnson is
known for creative choreography, strong musical interpretation, and
her ability to build a close performance partnership with her
celebrity contestants. Her work on DWTS has included a historic same-
sex pairing with JoJo Siwa, and she has won Mirrorballs with Adam
Rippon and Joey Graziadei.

CAREER HIGHLIGHTS
So You Think You Can Dance contestant; five-time U.S. National Latin
Champion; DWTS Mirrorball winner in Seasons 26 and 33.
