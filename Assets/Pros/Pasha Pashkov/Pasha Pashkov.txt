Pasha Pashkov was born in Russia and began ballroom dancing as a
child. He became an international competitor, often partnered with his
wife Daniella Karagach, and the pair appeared together on World of
Dance. Pashkov joined DWTS as a pro in Season 28 after years of
competitive and teaching experience. He is known for a precise
ballroom foundation and for choreographing routines that suit a
celebrity partner’s movement and personality. He continues to perform
on DWTS and its live tours.

CAREER HIGHLIGHTS
International ballroom competitor; World of Dance contestant; DWTS pro
since Season 28.
