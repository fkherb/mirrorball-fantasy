Witney Carson grew up in Utah and trained in ballroom, Latin, jazz,
and other dance styles. She became widely known as a contestant on So
You Think You Can Dance, then joined DWTS as a troupe member before
being promoted to pro. Carson has appeared in the show’s live tours
and is known for lively choreography and visually striking routines.
She has won two Mirrorballs: the first with Alfonso Ribeiro in Season
19 and the second with Robert Irwin in Season 34.

CAREER HIGHLIGHTS
