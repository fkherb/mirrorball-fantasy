Emma Slater grew up in England and began dancing as a child, training
in ballroom and Latin as well as performing in musical theater. Before
joining DWTS, she appeared in stage productions and dance projects in
the United Kingdom. She became a troupe member and then a pro,
developing a reputation for polished ballroom choreography and
expressive storytelling. Slater has also toured with DWTS and appeared
in television and stage performances outside the series. She won the
Mirrorball with football player Rashad Jennings in Season 24.

CAREER HIGHLIGHTS
Mama Mia! performer; UK-trained ballroom and Latin dancer; former DWTS
troupe member; Season 24 Mirrorball winner.
