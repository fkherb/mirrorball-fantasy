Brooklyn-born Daniella Karagach started dancing as a child and
developed into an accomplished ballroom and Latin competitor. She and
her husband, fellow pro Pasha Pashkov, competed together
internationally and appeared on World of Dance. Karagach joined DWTS
as a pro in Season 28. She is known for athletic routines, complex
lifts, and choreography that makes strong use of a partner’s
individual strengths. Her work with Iman Shumpert brought her a
Mirrorball Trophy and an Emmy nomination for choreography.

CAREER HIGHLIGHTS
International ballroom competitor; World of Dance contestant; Season
30 Mirrorball winner; Emmy-nominated choreographer.
