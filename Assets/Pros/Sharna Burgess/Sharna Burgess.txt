Sharna Burgess is an Australian dancer and choreographer who began
competing in ballroom at a young age. She has worked internationally
as a performer and teacher, including on Australian television dance
productions, before joining DWTS as a pro in Season 13. Burgess is
known for expressive choreography and for tailoring routines to
partners who may have little dance experience. She stepped away from
the show after Season 30 and later returned to the pro lineup. Her win
with Bobby Bones in Season 27 remains her first DWTS Mirrorball.

CAREER HIGHLIGHTS
International ballroom competitor; Season 27 Mirrorball winner;
returned to the pro cast for Season 35.
