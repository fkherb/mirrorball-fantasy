Jan Ravnik is a Slovenian dancer and choreographer whose career spans
ballroom, commercial dance, and live music performance. He toured with
Taylor Swift on the Eras Tour, performing in a major stadium
production seen by audiences around the world. Ravnik joined DWTS as a
pro in Season 34, bringing the staging experience of arena concerts
alongside his dance training. His work on the show includes partnering
celebrities and performing in ensemble numbers, where his background
in large-scale live shows is particularly useful.
