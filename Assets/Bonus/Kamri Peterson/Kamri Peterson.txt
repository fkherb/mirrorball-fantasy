Kamri Peterson is a Utah-based competitive dancer who has trained in
multiple styles and performed in national dance competitions. She has
described herself as a world dance champion and a 12-time national
finalist. Peterson joined the DWTS troupe for Season 35, becoming the
newest member of this lineup. Her background in competition gives her
experience with both technical training and high-pressure performance,
while the troupe role adds the fast-paced television choreography and
live-show experience that can lead to more prominent opportunities on
the series.
