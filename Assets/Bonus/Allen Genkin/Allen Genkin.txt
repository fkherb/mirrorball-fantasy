Allen Genkin is a Philadelphia-born ballroom dancer, choreographer,
and teacher who has spent decades in competitive dance. He competed on
Season 15 of So You Think You Can Dance, reaching the Top 20, and
later owned a dance studio in California. Genkin entered The Next Pro
with extensive ballroom experience and showed a personal, expressive
side in the show’s storytelling challenge. He has also spoken publicly
about overcoming cancer and how dance helped him through grief and
mental-health struggles.

CAREER HIGHLIGHTS
So You Think You Can Dance Season 15, Top 20; won the 2012 DWTS
Ballroom Battle; The Next Pro semifinalist.
