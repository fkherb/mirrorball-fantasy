Jaxon Willard is a Utah-born dancer who first came to broader
attention as a contestant on NBC’s World of Dance. His performance
background includes ballroom, contemporary, and commercial dance, and
he has performed in live and televised productions. Willard joined
DWTS as a troupe member in Season 34 and returned for Season 35. He
brings experience in competition and ensemble work to the troupe,
where performers must learn and stage routines quickly across a range
of styles.

CAREER HIGHLIGHTS
