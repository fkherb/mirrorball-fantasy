Onye Stevenson is a dancer and choreographer whose training includes
contemporary and commercial dance. Before joining DWTS, Stevenson
studied dance at the University of Southern California and built
experience through performances and choreography. She joined the DWTS
troupe in Season 34 and returned for Season 35. Troupe work gives her
a high-profile platform for ensemble dancing while drawing on the
versatility required to perform different styles and learn new
routines within a television production schedule.

CAREER HIGHLIGHTS
