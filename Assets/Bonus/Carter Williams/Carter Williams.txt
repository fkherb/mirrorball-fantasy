Carter Williams is a dancer and choreographer from Utah who trained in
competitive dance and has worked across ballroom, contemporary, and
commercial styles. He joined DWTS as a troupe member in Season 34,
appearing in ensemble performances and in the dance segments around
celebrity routines. Williams has also worked on live dance productions
and maintains a close connection with Utah’s dance community, where
several DWTS performers began their careers. His return to the troupe
for Season 35 continues his path from competition and stage work into
national television performance.

CAREER HIGHLIGHTS
