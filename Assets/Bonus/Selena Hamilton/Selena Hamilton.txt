Selena Hamilton is a commercial dancer whose background spans ballet,
tap, modern, jazz, and contemporary dance. She has worked in music and
fashion campaigns, including projects with KATSEYE, and performed as a
backup dancer for Doechii. Unlike contestants whose careers were
rooted primarily in ballroom competition, Hamilton brought commercial
performance experience and strong versatility to The Next Pro. Her run
on the show showcased her ability to adapt to ballroom styles,
choreograph for partners, and create routines with a contemporary,
music-video feel.
