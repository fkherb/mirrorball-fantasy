Erik Linder began ballroom dancing at age four and built a career as a
competitive dancer and performer with his longtime partner, Shelly
Linder. He competed on America’s Got Talent with Shelly, and later
reached the semifinals of So You Think You Can Dance with partner
Rickie Taylor. Linder’s work has extended beyond competition into
choreography, coaching, live performance, and television. On The Next
Pro, he paired with Selena Hamilton for a commercial-style salsa and
showed his partnership and performance skills across several
challenges.

CAREER HIGHLIGHTS
So You Think You Can Dance semifinalist; America’s Got Talent audition
