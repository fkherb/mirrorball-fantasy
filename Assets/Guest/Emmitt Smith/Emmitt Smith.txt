Emmitt Smith is the NFL's all-time leading rusher and one of the
greatest running backs in football history. After starring at the
University of Florida, he was drafted by the Dallas Cowboys in 1990
and spent 13 seasons in Dallas before finishing his career with the
Arizona Cardinals. He holds the league records for career rushing
yards, rushing touchdowns and rushing attempts.

Smith won three Super Bowls with the Cowboys and was named MVP of
Super Bowl XXVIII, capping a 1993 season in which he was also the
NFL's Most Valuable Player and rushing champion. He was inducted into
the Pro Football Hall of Fame in 2010.

On "Dancing with the Stars," Smith won the Mirrorball Trophy in Season
3 with Cheryl Burke and returned for the All-Stars season. He made an
appearance on Super Bowl Night.
