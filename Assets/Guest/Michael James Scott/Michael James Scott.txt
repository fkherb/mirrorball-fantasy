Michael James Scott is a Broadway actor and singer best known as the
longest-running Genie in Disney's "Aladdin," a role he played nearly
4,000 times on Broadway before passing the lamp in May 2026. He was part
of the show from its pre-Broadway run in Toronto, originated the Genie
in Australia, where he won the Helpmann Award, and also played the role
in London's West End and on the North American tour.

He originated the Minstrel in "Something Rotten!" and the Maggots Guy in
"The Book of Mormon," and his other Broadway credits include "Hair,"
"Elf" and "The Pirate Queen."

On Disney Night he performed "Under the Sea" from "The Little Mermaid"
for Ezra Frech's samba.
