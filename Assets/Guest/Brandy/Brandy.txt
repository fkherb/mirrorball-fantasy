Brandy Norwood, known simply as Brandy, is a Grammy-winning singer,
songwriter and actress. She broke out in the mid-1990s with hits
including "I Wanna Be Down" and "Have You Ever?", starred in the sitcom
"Moesha," and played the title role in the 1997 television film "Rodgers
& Hammerstein's Cinderella."

Her 1998 duet with Monica, "The Boy Is Mine," spent 13 weeks at No. 1 on
the Billboard Hot 100 and won the Grammy Award for Best R&B Performance
by a Duo or Group with Vocals. She competed on Season 11 of "Dancing
with the Stars" in 2010 with Maksim Chmerkovskiy, reaching the semi-
finals.

On Grammy Night she returned with Monica to sing "The Boy Is Mine" live
for Amber Glenn and Ezra Frech's rumba dance-off.
