Kyra Belle Johnson is a musical theater actress who stars as Belle in
Disney Theatrical Group's North American tour of "Beauty and the Beast,"
which opened at Proctors in Schenectady, New York, in June 2025. She
first got to know the show as a kid, playing a sugar cube in a school
production.

On Disney Night she sang "Belle" in the opening number and performed
"Supercalifragilisticexpialidocious" from "Mary Poppins" for Harry Shum
Jr.'s jazz.
