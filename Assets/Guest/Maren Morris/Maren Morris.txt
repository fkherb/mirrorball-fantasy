Maren Morris is a Grammy-winning singer-songwriter. Her major-label
debut single, "My Church," won the Grammy Award for Best Country Solo
Performance in 2017, and her albums include "Hero," "Girl" and "Humble
Quest." She is also a member of the country supergroup The Highwomen.

Her 2018 collaboration with Zedd and Grey, "The Middle," became a
worldwide hit and earned Grammy nominations for Record of the Year and
Song of the Year.

On Grammy Night she sang "The Middle" live for Jenna Dewan and Tatyana
Ali's cha-cha dance-off.
