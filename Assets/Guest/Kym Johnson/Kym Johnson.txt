Kym Johnson is a two-time "Dancing with the Stars" Mirrorball champion
and one of the most decorated professionals in the show's history.
Born in Sydney, Australia, she trained in ballroom and Latin from
childhood and toured internationally with the hit dance production
"Burn the Floor" before joining the original Australian "Dancing with
the Stars," where she won the second season.

She brought that success to the U.S. version, where she competed for
more than a decade, winning the Mirrorball with Donny Osmond in Season
9, which made her the first professional to win the competition in two
countries, and again with Hines Ward in Season 12. She also reached the
finals with Joey Fatone and Warren Sapp. After her time as a pro, she
served as a judge on the Australian series.

Johnson married her Season 20 partner, Robert Herjavec, in 2016, and
the couple has twins. She returned to "Dancing with the Stars" as a
guest judge for Dedication Night for the second year in a row.
