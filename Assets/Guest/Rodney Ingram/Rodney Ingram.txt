Rodney Ingram stars in the title role of Disney's "Aladdin" on Broadway.
Raised in the fishing village of Sayulita, Mexico, he finished high
school in Paris and trained at CAP21 in New York before making his
Broadway debut in 2015 in the "Aladdin" ensemble as the understudy for
Aladdin.

He went on to play Raoul in "The Phantom of the Opera" on Broadway,
originated Aladdin in the Spanish-language production in Mexico City,
and took over the role on Broadway in February 2026.

On Disney Night he performed "Be Our Guest" from "Beauty and the Beast"
for Jenna Dewan's quickstep and joined the troupe and Sherri Shepherd
for "Friend Like Me" from "Aladdin."
