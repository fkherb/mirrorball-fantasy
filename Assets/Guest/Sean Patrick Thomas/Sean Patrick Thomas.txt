Sean Patrick Thomas is an actor best known for playing Derek Reynolds
opposite Julia Stiles in "Save the Last Dance" (2001). His other films
include "Cruel Intentions" and "Barbershop," and he starred on the CBS
drama "The District."

On Super Bowl Night he made a cameo in his "Save the Last Dance" co-star
Julia Stiles's jazz.
