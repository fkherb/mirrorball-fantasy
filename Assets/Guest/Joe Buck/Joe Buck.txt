Joe Buck is one of the most recognizable voices in American sports
broadcasting and the lead play-by-play announcer for ESPN and ABC's
"Monday Night Football," a role he took on in 2022 alongside longtime
partner Troy Aikman. Before ESPN, he spent nearly three decades at Fox
Sports, where he became the network's lead voice for Major League
Baseball and the NFL, calling more than 20 World Series and six Super
Bowls.

The son of Hall of Fame broadcaster Jack Buck, Joe began his career
calling games while a student at Indiana University. He is an
eight-time Sports Emmy Award winner, a three-time National
Sportscaster of the Year and a member of the Sports Broadcasting Hall
of Fame, and he and his father are the first father and son to each
receive both the Pete Rozelle Radio-Television Award and the Ford C.
Frick Award. Outside the booth, he has hosted ESPN's "Jeopardy!" on
Disney+ and competed on "The Masked Singer."

He joined "Dancing with the Stars" as the guest judge for Super Bowl
Night.
