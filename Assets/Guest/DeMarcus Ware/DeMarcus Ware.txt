DeMarcus Ware is a Pro Football Hall of Fame pass rusher (Class of
2023) who starred for the Dallas Cowboys and the Denver Broncos. A
first-round pick out of Troy University in 2005, he became one of the
most feared defenders of his era: a nine-time Pro Bowler, four-time
first-team All-Pro and two-time NFL sacks leader. He capped his career
by winning Super Bowl 50 with the Broncos.

Ware competed in Season 27 of "Dancing with the Stars" with Lindsay
Arnold and made an appearance on Super Bowl Night.
