Danny Amendola is a two-time Super Bowl champion wide receiver known
for his toughness and clutch postseason play. After starring at Texas
Tech, he made his way into the NFL as an undrafted free agent and went
on to play for the St. Louis Rams, New England Patriots, Miami
Dolphins, Detroit Lions and Houston Texans. With the Patriots, he won
Super Bowls XLIX and LI and was the MVP of the AFC Championship Game
in the 2017 season.

Amendola competed in Season 33 of "Dancing with the Stars" with Witney
Carson, finishing fifth, and made an appearance on Super Bowl Night.
