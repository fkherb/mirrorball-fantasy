Donald Driver spent his entire 14-season NFL career with the Green Bay
Packers, rising from a seventh-round draft pick out of Alcorn State to
the franchise's all-time leader in receptions and receiving yards. A
multiple-time Pro Bowl wide receiver, he helped the Packers win Super
Bowl XLV.

Driver won the Mirrorball Trophy in Season 14 of "Dancing with the
Stars" with Peta Murgatroyd, and has since built a career as an
author, speaker and philanthropist. He made an appearance on Super
Bowl Night.
