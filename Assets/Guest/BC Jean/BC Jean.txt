BC Jean is a singer-songwriter best known for co-writing "If I Were a
Boy," which became a worldwide hit for Beyoncé. She is married to
"Dancing with the Stars" pro Mark Ballas, and the two perform together
as the duo Alexander Jean.

During the Season 35 premiere she sang "Black Velvet" live for Maura
Higgins and Mark Ballas's foxtrot.
