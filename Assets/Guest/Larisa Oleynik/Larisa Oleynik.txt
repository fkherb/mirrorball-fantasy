Larisa Oleynik is an actress who starred as the title character in
Nickelodeon's "The Secret World of Alex Mack" before playing Bianca
Stratford, the younger sister of Julia Stiles's Kat, in "10 Things I
Hate About You" (1999). Her later work includes "3rd Rock from the Sun"
and "Mad Men."

On Dedication Night she joined her "10 Things I Hate About You" co-stars
at the end of Julia Stiles's contemporary, dedicated to Heath Ledger.
