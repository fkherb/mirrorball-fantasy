Amber Riley is an actress and singer best known for playing Mercedes
Jones on "Glee," where she starred alongside Harry Shum Jr. She won
Season 17 of "Dancing with the Stars" in 2013 with Derek Hough, and in
2017 she won the Olivier Award for Best Actress in a Musical for playing
Effie White in "Dreamgirls" in London's West End.

On Mariah Carey Night she returned to sing "It's Like That" for her
former "Glee" co-star Harry Shum Jr.'s tango.
