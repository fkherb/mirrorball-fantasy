Joey Graziadei is a 28-year-old teaching tennis pro from Collegeville,
Pennsylvania, who stole hearts all over America on season 20 of “The
Bachelorette” with his loyalty, thoughtfulness and compassion.
Before his emotional exit, audiences saw Graziadei open up about his
family values and how those closest to him have influenced his desire
to find lasting love. As the Bachelor, Graziadei is looking for a life
partner who is outgoing, caring, and shares his love for adventure and
exploring the outdoors. He loves hiking, surfing and ending his days
watching the sunset but knows he has much more love to give, and the
only thing missing is someone with whom to share his life.
