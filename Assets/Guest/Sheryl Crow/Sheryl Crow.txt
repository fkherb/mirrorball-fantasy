Sheryl Crow is a nine-time Grammy-winning singer-songwriter and a 2023
inductee into the Rock & Roll Hall of Fame. Her debut album, "Tuesday
Night Music Club," produced the hit "All I Wanna Do," which won Grammy
Awards for Record of the Year and Best Female Pop Vocal Performance, and
she also won Best New Artist in 1995.

Her other hits include "If It Makes You Happy," "Everyday Is a Winding
Road" and "Soak Up the Sun."

On Grammy Night she sang "All I Wanna Do" live for Connor Wood and Maura
Higgins's cha-cha dance-off.
