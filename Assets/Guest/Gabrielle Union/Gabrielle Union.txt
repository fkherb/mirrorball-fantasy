Gabrielle Union is an actress and producer who played Chastity Church in
"10 Things I Hate About You" (1999). She went on to star in "Bring It
On" and "Bad Boys II," and to lead the series "Being Mary Jane" and
"L.A.'s Finest."

On Dedication Night she joined her "10 Things I Hate About You" co-stars
at the end of Julia Stiles's contemporary, dedicated to Heath Ledger.
