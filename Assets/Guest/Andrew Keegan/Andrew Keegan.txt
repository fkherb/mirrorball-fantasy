Andrew Keegan is an actor who played Joey Donner in "10 Things I Hate
About You" (1999). He was also known in the late 1990s for "7th Heaven"
and "Party of Five," and appeared in the film "O."

On Dedication Night he joined his "10 Things I Hate About You" co-stars
at the end of Julia Stiles's contemporary, dedicated to Heath Ledger.
