Heather Headley is a Trinidadian-American singer and actress who
originated Nala in Disney's "The Lion King" on Broadway in 1997. Three
years later she created the title role in Elton John and Tim Rice's
"Aida," winning the Tony Award for Best Actress in a Musical.

Her debut album, "This Is Who I Am" (2002), earned her a Grammy
nomination for Best New Artist, and she later won a Grammy for her
gospel album "Audience of One." She has also starred as Rachel Marron in
"The Bodyguard" in London's West End and as Shug Avery in the Broadway
revival of "The Color Purple."

On Disney Night she sang in the opening number and performed "Easy as
Life" from "Aida" for Tatyana Ali's contemporary and "I See the Light"
from "Tangled" for Connor Wood's waltz.
