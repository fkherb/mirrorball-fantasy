Monica Arnold, known simply as Monica, is a Grammy-winning R&B singer
who signed her first record deal at 12 and released her debut album,
"Miss Thang," in 1995. Her hits include "Don't Take It Personal (Just
One of Dem Days)," "Angel of Mine" and "So Gone."

Her 1998 duet with Brandy, "The Boy Is Mine," spent 13 weeks at No. 1 on
the Billboard Hot 100 and won the Grammy Award for Best R&B Performance
by a Duo or Group with Vocals.

On Grammy Night she returned with Brandy to sing "The Boy Is Mine" live
for Amber Glenn and Ezra Frech's rumba dance-off.
