Joseph Gordon-Levitt is an actor, filmmaker and the founder of the
collaborative production company HitRecord. He played Cameron James
opposite Julia Stiles in "10 Things I Hate About You" (1999) after
starring on the sitcom "3rd Rock from the Sun," and went on to films
including "(500) Days of Summer," "Inception," "Looper" and "The Dark
Knight Rises." He wrote and directed "Don Jon."

On Dedication Night he joined his "10 Things I Hate About You" co-stars
at the end of Julia Stiles's contemporary, dedicated to Heath Ledger.
