Emmy® Award winner Julianne Hough is a true triple threat — dancer,
actress, singer and born entertainer. Known globally for her breakout
on “Dancing with the Stars,” she’s the youngest pro to win
twice, judge on the panel, and now host the hit ABC series.

On screen, Hough has starred in “Burlesque,” “Footloose,”
“Rock of Ages,” “Safe Haven” and “Grease Live!” — and
made her Broadway debut in 2022 in the critically acclaimed
“POTUS.”

Beyond the stage, she founded KINRGY, a movement and wellness platform
empowering self-expression through dance. With a flagship studio in
West Hollywood and worldwide digital access, KINRGY is growing fast.
She also co-founded Ovation, a next-gen dance convention and
competition tour, alongside brother Derek Hough and DanceOne.

With several dance-focused projects in the works, Hough returns to
film in 2026 in Maggie Gyllenhaal’s “The Bride” — once again
blending movement and storytelling at the highest level.
