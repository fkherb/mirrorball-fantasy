Susan May Pratt is an actress who played Mandella, Kat Stratford's best
friend, in "10 Things I Hate About You" (1999). Her other films include
"Center Stage" and "Drive Me Crazy."

On Dedication Night she joined her "10 Things I Hate About You" co-stars
at the end of Julia Stiles's contemporary, dedicated to Heath Ledger.
