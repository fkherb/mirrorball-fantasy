Hayley Erbert is a dancer who reached the top ten of "So You Think You
Can Dance" before joining the "Dancing with the Stars" troupe. She
married judge Derek Hough in 2023, and the two perform together on tour,
including in their Symphony of Dance show.

During the Season 35 premiere she danced in Derek Hough's Symphony of
Dance number alongside dancers from the tour.

PERSONAL INFORMATION
HOMETOWN: Topeka, Kansas
