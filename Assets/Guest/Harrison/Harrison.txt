Harrison is a London-based DJ, producer and vocalist. His rework of
t.A.T.u.'s "All the Things She Said" went to No. 1 on Spotify's Viral
chart in the U.S. and Canada after it was featured in "Heated Rivalry,"
and he is the creator of the Secret Rivalry Rave party series.

On Viral Hits Night he performed his version of "All the Things She
Said" live for Amber Glenn's tango.
