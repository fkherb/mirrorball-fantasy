Sherri Shepherd is a comedian, actress and television host. She was a
co-host of ABC's "The View" from 2007 to 2014, sharing a Daytime Emmy
Award for Outstanding Talk Show Host, and has hosted her own daytime
talk show, "Sherri," since 2022. Her acting credits include "Less than
Perfect" and "30 Rock."

She competed on Season 14 of "Dancing with the Stars" in 2012, partnered
with Val Chmerkovskiy. On Disney Night she returned to join the troupe
and Rodney Ingram for "Friend Like Me" from "Aladdin."
