Lochlan "Lucky" Howard is a six-time world champion Irish step dancer
and professional touring performer. He grew up in New Albany, Ohio,
and trained at The Academy Irish Dance Co. in Westerville, Ohio,
winning six individual World Irish Dance Association championships
along with multiple team titles.

Howard has toured and performed with productions including "Lord of
the Dance" and has collaborated with groups such as the Fusion
Fighters. He shares his dancing, lifestyle and amusement park content
on Instagram, TikTok and YouTube.

On Dedication Night, he joined Maura Higgins and Mark Ballas for their
paso doble dedicated to Ireland, Maura's home country.
