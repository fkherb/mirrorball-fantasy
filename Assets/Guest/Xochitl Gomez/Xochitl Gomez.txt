Xochitl Gomez is an actress best known for playing America Chavez in
Marvel's "Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness" and Dawn
Schafer in Netflix's "The Baby-Sitters Club." She began acting as a
child, making her screen debut on Disney Channel's "Raven's Home."

At 17, she won Season 32 of "Dancing with the Stars" with Val
Chmerkovskiy, becoming the first winner of the Len Goodman Mirrorball
Trophy. She voices Gabby in the animated film "The Cat in the Hat"
(2026), and appeared in the troupe's "CATastrophe" number on
Dedication Night.
